## About This Game

### Intro

Signal Chain is a cyberpunk roguelite deckbuilder built around spatial chain combat. Place cards on a 5x5 grid, choose where the chain begins, and unleash step-by-step attacks, defenses, status effects, and combo payoffs. Every placement matters, because your arrows determine how the whole turn unfolds.

### Twist

Between attacks, the board persists, letting you reshape your setup over multiple actions in the same turn. Off-chain cards still contribute, curses punish bad routing, and enemies telegraph attacks, traps, locks, and debuffs that force you to adapt on the fly.

### Run-structuur

Push through a branching run map filled with street fights, signal events, shops, and bosses. Grow your deck, install powerful body mods, and find a build that can survive all the way to the Warden.

## Features

- **Spatial Deckbuilding:** Build attacks on a 5x5 board where card arrows determine the chain route.
- **Multi-Attack Turns:** The board persists between attacks, creating evolving turns instead of one-and-done actions.
- **Cyberpunk Buildcraft:** Upgrade your deck and install body mods that reshape your strategy.
- **Telegraphed Enemy Pressure:** React to attacks, traps, siphons, locks, and other enemy intents before they trigger.
- **Branching Roguelite Runs:** Choose your path through battles, events, shops, and bosses in seeded runs with high replayability.
